Energy systems · M.S. graduate project

Game-theoretic energy management

Spring 2024

Combining wind power forecasting with coordinated microgrid energy management.

My contribution

Developed the wind forecasting component for an energy management framework modeled as a three-stage Stackelberg game.

  • Implemented a genetic stacked autoencoder forecasting model in MATLAB.
  • Optimized the deep learning model and analyzed the effect of forecast accuracy on energy management.
  • Worked within a framework coordinating the utility, storage company, and users through dynamic pricing.
MATLABDeep learningGame theoryWind forecasting

Project framework: hierarchical energy coordination and pricing using backward induction.
